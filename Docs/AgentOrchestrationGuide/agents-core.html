01 · Context formation

Understand the request before acting.

These agents route the request, extract meaning, retrieve compact evidence, find candidate devices, and reduce ambiguity to canonical targets and capabilities.

How context accumulates

The direct-command graph uses two fan-out waves, then a sequential selection spine.

rootOperation routeOperation, language, and domain enter shared context.
wave 1 · parallelNLUSemantic intent/device type, slots, and risk are inferred independently.
wave 2 · parallelEvidenceCapability, Bixby, examples, and device candidates are retrieved.
joinJudge & rankRetrieval quality is checked and candidate IDs are selected.
canonicalizeHydrate & resolveFull device records and a capability/command decision are produced.
Advisory versus authoritative

Model and RAG outputs guide the search. Device hydration, capability catalogs, and later safety gates restore canonical authority before any execution plan is accepted.
